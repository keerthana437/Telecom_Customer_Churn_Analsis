# Telecom Customer Churn Analysis | Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn
• Executed an end-to-end exploratory analysis on 5,000 telecom customer records across 19 features, validating data hygiene to confirm zero
missing entries and zero duplicate records.
• Quantified baseline customer churn at 13% (649 accounts) representing ~$42K in annualized revenue exposure (14% of total revenue).
• Isolated customer service interactions as the primary leading indicator: 17.2% churn for 4+ service calls versus 8.8% for 0–3 calls (~2x risk
factor).
• Uncovered high-risk segments: International Plan subscribers (18.2% vs. 12.0%) and top-quartile daytime minutes users (20.3% vs. 10.5%);
formulated proactive retention outreach triggers after 4 service calls.
• Generated distribution plots, correlation heatmaps, and categorical visual breakdowns in Seaborn/Matplotlib to present business findings.
